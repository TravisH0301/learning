## Vocal Foundation
### Rate of speech
- Slow down when speaking important words/sentences to obtain attention
- Small pause before the word to build anticipation

### Volume
- Increase volume to highlight keypoints and convey emotion

### Tonality
- Add emotion to speaking by making facial expression with varying speed, volume and tone. This makes it more connecting.
- Empathy/Warmth: Use a quiet, slow, and higher-pitched tone
- Authority/Confidence: Use a lower, steady, and deliberate pace
- Excitement/Energy: Use a faster, louder, and more varied tone

### Pause
- Pause to give time to process after something complex & important
- Pause instead of non-words (e.g., umm) and filler words (e.g., and, so)
